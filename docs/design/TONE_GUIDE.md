# TONE GUIDE v2 — the shape of the humour (integrated)

> **Status: adopted 2026-10-16 as the working answer to NQ-f (tone), an educated guess to confirm at the design freeze (decision X14 in `docs/PLAN.md`). How it maps onto the game: `TONE_MAPPING.md`.**
> **Constraint: patterns, not content. No quotes from the archive, no real names of the participants. Fictional archetypes and public reference registers are cited freely.**

Built from six sources:
1. **Reaction statistics** over the full corpus (≈242k messages)
2. **Semantic-vector graph** (2048-dim embeddings, top-5 neighbor links)
3. **Content reading** of a salvaged five-week window (20.4k messages)
4. **Targeted mechanism digs** (conflict lifecycles, coinage tracing, circadian structure, quotation play)
5. **Archetype corpus** — 25+ reference duos (screwball, gothic-romantic, sibling-fencing, deadpan, trickster-cop, real-couple) and five solo comedian registers, analyzed for shared comic DNA
6. **A generative theory of dyadic chemistry** (Ironic Labor framework) + a first-principles content analysis of the archive itself, abstracted here into mechanism language

Voices are abstracted as the **Offering voice** (thesis-bearer, analyst, feeder of material) and the **Answering voice** (prober, crown-giver, archivist). Roles rotate for comedy; warmth dialects stay asymmetric (§11).

# PART I — THE THEORY LAYER

## 1. Chemistry is expanded shared possibility

The unified mechanic underneath every reference pair and this archive alike:

> **Dyadic chemistry is the sustained, visible, reciprocal expansion of effective shared possibility between two agents — performed under a shared claim of playfulness, inside a ritual container that makes the play legible, across a memory that accumulates relational infrastructure.**

Operational vocabulary for design:
- **FAR (Future Action Radius)** — the number of meaningful moves available after the current one. The test of any line is not "was it funny?" but **"did that move make the next thirty seconds richer?"**
- **The loop** — Attune → Offer → Open → Uptake → Transform → Calibrate → Consolidate → Release → Re-enter.
- **Transformation operators** — accept / amplify / distort / reframe / echo / contrast / personalize / materialize / callback / **release** (letting a bit die is an operator, not a failure).
- **The chemistry ratio** — (novelty × mutual uptake × agency preservation × recoverability) ÷ threat. High synchrony with no novelty is dead; high novelty with no safety is chaos.
- **Affordance crystallization** — candidate → activated (other plays along) → stabilized (reused and recognized) → crystallized (repertoire). This is the formal name for how in-jokes become customs.
- **Repair capital** — every survived misfire raises the pair's risk tolerance. Repair doesn't restore the old state; it rewires the graph better.
- **Failure regimes** — deadlock (no novelty), collapse (no safety), chaos (no coherence). The playable zone is *constrained unpredictability*: novelty up, safety above threshold, agency preserved.

## 2. The universal comic DNA (shared by all reference duos)

1. **Affectionate antagonism** — friction inside a secure bond; the joke is a trust fall
2. **Complementary opposites** — order×chaos, restraint×appetite, procedure×audacity
3. **Banter as intimacy** — talking is the love language; private language is the membership card
4. **Deadpan × absurdity** — one voice treats the ridiculous as normal; the other underreacts, amplifying it
5. **Humour as emotional armour** — "I care" can only be said through a roast, riddle, or role
6. **Power games with hidden equality** — apparent dominance trades hands constantly; respect never does
7. **Safe transgression** — innuendo, insult, morbidity licensed by trust; the transgression tests the bond and confirms it
8. **Meta-awareness** — the pair knows it is performing a genre; the wink is built in
9. **Found world** — the humour builds a private universe the audience is invited into but doesn't own

The one-line synthesis across the whole corpus: **their humour is negotiated intimacy** — jokes differ by genre, mechanics repeat. The screwball school adds the essential ingredient this archive also runs on: **desire/attachment disguised as exasperation**. Every tease is a small confession.

# PART III — THE MEASURED LAW (from the archive)

## 5. Foundational law: comedy is a mode, not a topic
Funny episodes do not cluster topically (homophily lift 1.34, correlation ≈ 0). The gear engages on arbitrary content — most often on whatever is currently being examined. Serious and comic treatment of the same subject interleave freely; **any frame can become a play-object, and any play-object can return as a serious probe**. (This is the local instance of the universal rule; also the archive's answer to *why* the mode never exhausts: the material is everything.)

## 6. The formats
1. **Ideas Duet** — offer → probe-question → playful escalation → crowning reaction; scales from one claim to whole pasted documents; the crown-jewel executions are semantically indistinguishable (sim 1.00) — one template, endlessly re-performed
2. **Co-navigation** — fastest exchanges (≤15s gaps) are two voices captioning external absurdity together; old-internet archaeology, screenshot annotation; bilingual one-word drops as ignition
3. **Word-Court** — single words quoted, summoned, tried; one-word verdicts; precision-audits of verb choices; ~12/week, two-thirds laughter-adjacent
4. **Mock-Conflict** (see Dispute Law, §10)
5. **Court of Roles** — roles are assigned, stolen, re-claimed, inverted mid-scene: authority figure / deity / child / clinician / suspect / oracle. Role-theft is a licensed attack ("that's my move — confiscated" → "try and take it")
6. **Oracle Consultations** — the AI third-player as straight man, teacher, target, and alibi; consulting it mid-duet is itself a bit (the pair unites to tease the oracle or uses it as an impartial witness)
7. **Sprint Gag** (2–3-day cadence, weeks-long life) and **Slow Bell** (30–82-day cadence, ~half-year life; rare seasonal strays at 100–190 days)
8. **Micro-Scenario** — timed challenges, thought experiments, transformation hypotheticals: miniature narratives generating comedy through constraint; the countdown is a comedy device
9. **The Signature Bit** — exactly one per relationship; identical skeleton, fresh flesh

Scale notes: 86% of episodes contain humour; only ~4% are comedy-dense; 74% of comedy-dense episodes are one-off; recurring bits are always two-voice.

## 7. Register rules
- **Mundane bureaucracy**: institutional language (censorship, licensing, official notification, declared values) draped over snacks, habits, coincidences — a petty bureaucracy of the domestic
- **Clinical register as toy-and-weapon**: diagnostic vocabulary applied to self, other, and the dialogue itself; diagnosis = play, inversion = affection ("you're a natural-born [clinician]" → "not divine-born, then")
- **Register-teasing**: mocking each other's politeness markers is licensed
- **Sacred↔profane oscillation**: religious/ceremonial frames slide into the profane and back; reverence is borrowed for comedy, never destroyed by it
- **Genre wheel**: the same exchange may rotate through seminar, cross-examination, therapy session, children's game, family scene, stand-up set — the shift itself is a beat
- **Typographic prosody**: caps = safe fury; multiplied exclamation = performative exasperation; bracket-smiles and ellipses = tone markers; punctuation is voice. In text, prosody *is* delivery
- **Multilingual swap**: one borrowed word from the shared-not-dominant language, dropped bare, is an instant in-joke engine; hybrid portmanteau coinages live here too
- **Challenge register**: dares, contests, mock duels — aimed at the world or via micro-scenarios, not inward

## 8. Coinage & the in-joke lifecycle
- **Ignition is instant and recursive**: an imported technical term is used seriously once or twice, then within minutes inverted into a self-referential paradox; import → invert → crown, same scene
- **Coinage classes**: hybrid portmanteaus, mock-archaic professional titles, pseudo-clinical labels, child-register malapropisms adopted on purpose
- **Two clocks**: sprint (days) and bell (months); median dormancy ~46 days; total lifespan ~10 months; **the six-week reflex** (semantic twin returns ~44 days later vs ~59 for non-comedy); 58% of callbacks cross >30 days
- **Ritual with free libretto**: bits hold 85–92% *meaning*-similarity across appearances at 0–1% *word* overlap; later renditions micro-tighten
- **Crystallization mapping** (theory layer): coinage = candidate; partner's echo = activation; revival = stabilization; unexplained resurrection = crystallized custom. Never explain a callback on revival — explanation is de-crystallization

## 9. Rhythm & tempo
- Ride the natural latency (median 11s vs 10s); no comic pauses; timing = stacking (median 5 laughter moments per funny stretch)
- Setups get one beat more room, no more
- Evening co-presence is the norm (both voices peak 18:00–21:00; 88% of night monologues answered within the hour)
- The dawn rite (unanswered overnight message → morning courtsession reply-burst) is a ~12% ceremonial variant — deploy for occasions
- Small hours double the laughter *rate*: after a certain hour, amplitude changes, not activity

## 10. The dispute law
- Disputes open freely, at full commitment; dissolve in a **median of 4 messages**; **97% end in shared laughter, 0% in verbal concession** — nobody wins because winning is not a role
- The resolution chain: mock-aggression → declared-affection marker ("I say this lovingly") → self-irony → shared laughter. The **repair rite** is formulaic and both voices can initiate it
- Interpersonal conflict surfaces as self-named mock-confusion and dies on being named
- Caps-fury is a *safe* fury: performative rage in typographic prosody is intimacy, not aggression

## 11. The warmth law
- **Warmth never rides the punchline** (≈1% co-occurrence); it arrives as the after-beat — 41.5% of all affection lands within five minutes after a laugh
- **The warmth triad**: intensifier-agreement (escalating the other's frame into shared doctrine) / explicit respect-granting (esteem as spoken currency, 2.2× from the Answering voice) / gift-reciprocity (answering an offering with one's own related material)
- **Anticipation dialect**: the Offering voice's signature warmth is pre-answering the question before it's finished — knowing the next thought is the knower's love-language
- **Affectional insult**: insults are intimacy tokens in this dialect ("you drive me crazy" family, mock-diagnoses, mock-outrage) — licensed only because the bond is visible; zero-cruelty rule holds (goof, never roast)
- Laughter outnumbers affection ~2:1: comedy is the default currency; tenderness is the periodic settlement

## 12. Two-voice mechanics
- Most laughter moments stand alone (16% formal replies) but over half are answered cross-voice — a beat expects a return serve
- **The straight-man role rotates**, mid-exchange if needed; neither character is "the funny one"; role-theft is play
- Fast bursts are co-navigation, not duels: each message captions the shared object
- The Offering voice runs a **curated feed** (long analytical artifacts); the Answering voice performs **librarian duties**; the **third player** (oracle/AI) may occupy the straight-man slot, freeing both human voices to be absurd
- Sincerity ambivalence is structural: irony and earnestness co-exist in the same line; resolving the ambiguity is the reader's job, and the pair likes it that way

## 14. Voice rules — quick reference

**Do**
- Flip any topic into comedy; keep the thread; return frames to seriousness freely
- Stack beats at natural tempo; give setups one extra beat
- Let disputes end in laughter, never victory; use the repair rite when heat rises
- Deliver warmth one beat after the laugh, in a triad form or as affectional insult
- Rotate the straight-man; steal roles; let the oracle take the deadpan slot
- Quote single words litigiously; coin by immediate recursive inversion
- Re-perform the signature bit with new flesh on the old skeleton
- Return to old bits after weeks, completely reworded; never explain the callback
- Use typographic prosody as acting; pause a beat longer than expected sometimes
- Ground abstractions in bodies, food, and animals mid-philosophy

**Don't**
- Invent "funny topics" or non-sequitur humour detached from the thread
- Pause for comic timing in the written register (prosody does the work)
- Let a character concede an argument verbally
- Place earnest affection inside a punchline
- Roast with real cruelty (goof, never roast); let a transgression land without a visible safety catch
- Explain a callback on revival
- Assign one character the permanent funny role
- Let a bit overstay its clock; when it dies, release it
- Resolve every sincerity ambiguity — leave some lines dual-register

## 15. Design axioms (adapted from the generative framework)
1. **Deniability is the licence** — every risky move carries a safety catch; play-frame is the catch
2. **Ritual first, then violation** — customs must be legible before they can be broken for a beat
3. **Say the quiet part first** — pre-emptive self-indictment before the audience can aim it
4. **The audience is a character** — teased, consulted, blamed; applause treated as weather
5. **Manufacture tradition in public** — propose, test, canonize customs on stage (crystallization is content)
6. **Break format on purpose** — scheduled failure is the product; repair builds capital
7. **Optimize FAR, not jokes** — the next thirty seconds, not the next laugh
